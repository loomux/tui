### Added

- `loomux` with no command shows the list of conversations on the
  profile's server, attention-first like the web: needs you, running,
  then done, newest first. `tab` shows one group at a time, `/` filters
  as you type and `?` shows the keys in effect. The list polls every 5
  seconds (LOOM-283).
- The keymap's `apply` action (`enter`) keeps the list's filter; `back`
  (`esc`) clears it (LOOM-283).
